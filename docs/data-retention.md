# Data Retention

Default suggested periods:

- Incomplete leads: 30 days after last activity.
- Rejected or unsafe requests: 30 days, with minimal audit metadata where legally necessary.
- Attachments: 30 days after completion or 90 days after upload, whichever is earlier, unless Jawad explicitly extends a job record.
- Group demand excerpts: 30 days maximum by default.
- Completed commercial records: follow Jawad's accounting/legal requirements; avoid retaining unnecessary message content.
- Audit and payment-assignment evidence: retain as required for dispute and accounting integrity, without private keys or wallet secrets.

Users can request deletion. Deletion must remove attachments and personal/intake content while preserving only narrowly necessary financial/audit records. A worker job performs retention deletions and records the action without reproducing deleted content.
