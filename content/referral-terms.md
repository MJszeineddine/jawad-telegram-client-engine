# Referral Terms

- Default suggested commission: 20% of collected service revenue.
- Eligibility begins only after delivery and client acceptance.
- Refunds, chargebacks, duplicate/self-referrals, fraud, unauthorised promotions, spam, or false claims can remove eligibility.
- Payout is manually reviewed and made outside the application.
- Partners may not claim guaranteed results, impersonate Jawad, or publish wallet addresses without approval.
- The latest written agreement controls any conflict with this suggested template.
