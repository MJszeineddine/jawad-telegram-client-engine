# Privacy Notice

Jawad Dev Desk collects only information needed to evaluate and deliver a voluntarily submitted engineering request: Telegram identity basics, contact preference, project/task details, attachments, attribution, quote/job state, and payment-verification evidence.

Do not submit passwords, tokens, `.env` files, private keys, seed or recovery phrases, production database dumps, or information you are not authorised to share.

The system does not sell personal data, harvest contacts, scrape private groups, or privately message group authors who have not started the bot. Authorised group monitoring sends excerpts only to Jawad and follows configurable retention.

Deletion requests remove personal content and attachments subject to narrowly necessary financial, dispute, fraud-prevention, and audit obligations.
